Mission & Vision

Aid in education and vocational training

F.C. Sondhi Charitable Trust came into existence on 2nd of May 1978. The major aims and objectives of the Trust are to provide help to the needy, particularly in the fields of education and vocational training.

The Trust does this by studying applications from various institutions and individuals and sanctioning scholarships and grants in conformity with the above mentioned ideals.

Our Founder

Mr. F. C. Sondhi

A great philanthropist and self made businessman, born in 1906 in Sialkot, now in Pakistan.

ChildhoodBoth his parents died when he was three years old and he was left to grow up in the home of some relatives.
BeliefsFrom a very young age he believed in hard, diligent and honest study and at the same time was deeply committed to the upliftment of the down trodden. He was an active member of the Arya Samaj movement in those days. Through his own hard work and self education he was able to do well in his academic studies and was called by the, then major sports company in United India, M/s Uberoi Limited to work there. He developed a shining reputation for honesty and was put under the personal supervision of Mr. Uberoi himself.
CareerIn 1936 he was sent to England and eventually settled down there. However he had a great yearning to visit his motherland and came to India often. From 1967 (when his younger son came and settled in Jalandhar) till 1983 (when he passed away), he used to visit India every year for periods of three to six months at a time and got deeply involved in projects for helping the poor and the needy. He was also here for one full year in 1972-1973, as President of the local Rotary Club.
PhilosophyLate Mr. F. C. Sondhi was a spiritually minded philanthropist and self made businessman. He believed in treating all human beings as equal, irrespective of caste and creed. He also believed in a universal religion without dogma. He was also a great believer in action, which was exemplified by his life work. That is why Trust was started.

Scholarships

Education for every genuine case

F.C. Sondhi Charitable Trust has been giving scholarships for the last 40 years or so. First we used to offer scholarships to all school going children, but then realized that cost of education in lower classes is nominal. We now entertain applications from 9th class to M.A.

Each application is verified by a visit to the home of the student, to confirm the facts entered on the fairly comprehensive application form. Our aim is to help all genuine cases who come to us.

Many of our scholarships are sponsored by donors. We have a policy that every paisa of donated money will be issued for the scholarship, and all administration costs will be borne by the Trust.

Grants to the needy

Support for the elderly and widows

There are surprisingly, even in India where the joint family prevails, many elderly ladies and even a few elderly gentlemen who have absolutely no income and very little support. Their families have deserted them and they do not have the capacity to work any longer. Most of these people are over the age of 60.

After starting the scholarship scheme, it came to our notice that widows faced many problems. A scheme was started to give grants to widows. Our aim is to help elderly widows and other destitute people and we are giving financial aid of Rs. 850/- or Rs. 1,000/- per month at present. Since the beneficiaries are quite aged, we have devised a scheme so that they receive their grant at their doorsteps.

Ideas Exchange

A newsletter since the beginning

The Trust also runs a Newsletter which has been regularly published since the inception of the Trust. The Newsletter by the name of "Ideas Exchange" contains spiritual, moral and general interest articles condensed into an easily readable form for our readers.

Our founder, Late Sh. F.C. Sondhi, worked hard to start the "Ideas Exchange" on a regular basis and in the early days he did all the work of compiling articles which could be of interest to readers. As the name of the Magazine indicates, Sh. F.C. Sondhi was keen that this little magazine could become a forum where the readers would write in their various different ideas and that it would become a medium for exchange of ideas between people all over the world.

It currently has a circulation of about 1000 members, spread all over India and other countries. Mr. F.C. Sondhi's grand-daughter (Gitanjali) was the editor for 20 years.

Trust activities & updates

What we are doing now

October - November - December 2026

Our commitment — financial and otherwise

During the current financial year, the Trust has planned an estimated expenditure of ₹70 lakh towards grants, scholarships and other charitable initiatives, with an aim to benefit more than 850 deserving beneficiaries.

Up to August 2026, an amount of ₹36 lakh has already been disbursed, including ₹24 lakh towards grants and financial assistance, and ₹12 lakh towards scholarships for deserving students.

For the remaining part of the financial year, a further ₹34 lakh has been earmarked for various commitments, including ₹24.5 lakh towards grants and ₹8.5 lakh towards college scholarships.

We remain committed to supporting both existing and new beneficiaries through our grant and scholarship programmes. Our endeavour is to ensure that assistance reaches deserving individuals in a timely, efficient and transparent manner, while continuing our efforts to make a meaningful difference in their lives.

Photo from Charitable Trust Function held in March 2025

July - September 2026

As reported in The Tribune on 15th May 2026, sisters Prabhjot Kaur and Veerpal Kaur from Village Rampur Gaura achieved outstanding academic results despite their family suffering severe losses in the August 2025 floods. Prabhjot scored 88.2% in Class 12 and Veerpal secured 88.7% in Class 10.

To encourage them to continue their education, F.C. Sondhi Charitable Trust has awarded scholarships to both girls. While we understand that financial assistance alone cannot solve all the challenges faced by the family, we hope this support provides some encouragement and relief. Subject to satisfactory progress, these scholarships will continue each year for as long as they remain in education.

April - June 2026

As part of current year's initiatives, Trust has introduced a focused programme to promote vocational education among students completing 10th and 10+2, encouraging a shift from classroom learning to skill-based careers. A scholarship scheme for vocational courses has been rolled out across 8-9 government schools. Students are being encouraged to explore skill-based career options, and application forms are available through Trust office.

With effect from 1st April, monthly grant has been increased from ₹750/- to ₹850/- for all beneficiaries. Beneficiaries with special needs or exceptional circumstances will continue to receive ₹1,000/- per month. Trust remains committed to supporting practical education and enabling beneficiaries to build sustainable livelihoods.
